Industries

Built for the people who do the work

ScrubHub started in cleaning and is built for any small business that sends people to a client's home or property.

🧽

Cleaning

Residential and commercial cleaning, move-in/move-out and deep cleans.

Recurring visits, room-by-room checklists and client notes like gate codes and pets keep every clean consistent.

🔧

Plumbing

Service calls, repairs and installs.

Send an estimate from the job site, schedule the work, track parts on each truck and collect payment before you leave.

🌿

Lawn Care & Landscaping

Mowing routes, seasonal cleanups and installs.

Set weekly or bi-weekly recurring jobs, assign crews and bill repeat clients automatically.

🔨

Handyman

Repairs, installs and odd jobs.

Quote fast from your services price list, keep photos and notes on the job and invoice from your phone.

💦

Pressure Washing

Driveways, decks, roofs and siding.

Turn estimates into scheduled jobs, keep checklists for each surface and get paid online.

Pool Service

Weekly maintenance and repairs.

Recurring routes, chemical and supply tracking, and a portal where clients see every visit.

HVAC

Maintenance plans, tune-ups and repairs.

Schedule seasonal maintenance, track filters and parts, and keep the service history for every client.

Pest Control

Recurring treatments and one-time services.

Quarterly or monthly recurring visits, treatment checklists and automatic reminders for clients.
