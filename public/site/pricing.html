Billing questions

How does the free trial work?

You get full access for 14 days. No credit card is needed to start. At the end of the trial, pick the plan that fits.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly?

Same features. Yearly billing costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime and the change applies to your next bill.

Are there any other fees?

There are no setup fees or contracts. When your clients pay you by card, Stripe charges its standard processing fee on each payment.

What counts as a team member?

Anyone on your staff who logs in: owners, admins and employees. Your clients never count toward the limit.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account whenever you want. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
