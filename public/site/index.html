Questions? We've got answers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ScrubHub runs in any web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You and your team can also add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial, and you don't need a credit card to start.

Can my employees and clients use it too?

Yes. You invite them with a link. Employees get their own view with their jobs and checklists. Clients get a portal to see appointments, invoices and pay online.

How do I get paid?

Send an invoice and your client pays by card through a secure link or the client portal. Payments are processed by Stripe and go to your bank account. Standard Stripe processing fees apply.

Can I use my own logo and colors?

Yes. Your workspace, login page and client portal can show your logo and brand colors.

What kinds of businesses is ScrubHub for?

Small home-service businesses: cleaning, plumbing, lawn care, handyman, pressure washing, pool service, HVAC, pest control and similar trades.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Anytime. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your account. No contracts.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure and every connection is encrypted. Card payments are handled by Stripe, so card numbers never touch our servers.
